3 · Scorecard

The import fills this in and verifies it against the card's own totals — it stays tucked away unless something needs a look. Anything the reader wasn't sure about is highlighted for a quick check. S strokes · • handicap strokes on the hole (the dots — subtracted for Robin low ball & sum) · P putts · FW fairway · GR green in regulation.

4 · Robin pairings

Default rotation: 1&2 v 3&4 → 1&3 v 2&4 → 1&4 v 2&3 every six holes. Adjust to match who actually played together.

5 · Closest to the pin

Pick the winner on each par 3 (leave blank if nobody hit the green).

6 · Results

Import a round (or enter scores) and results appear here.

All pools zero-sum: what winners collect equals what the group put in. Robin settles directly between players at the $/point rate on point differences. If screenshot import can't start (no internet or a blocked network), scores can always be entered by hand.
